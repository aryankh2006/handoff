# 2-minute pitch outline

**0:00 – Hook (15s)**
"Right now in California, ambulances wait in ER hallways — sometimes for hours — with patients still
on the stretcher. Every minute a crew waits is a minute they're not answering the next 911 call."

**0:15 – Problem (25s)**
- AB 40 sets a 30-minute ambulance patient offload standard. Many hospitals miss it.
- ERs often don't know a wave of ambulances is coming until they're on the ramp.
- Radio reports are verbal, unstructured, and get lost.

**0:40 – Solution (20s)**
Handoff: paramedics just call a phone number and talk. AI turns it into a structured card on the
ER board. When 3+ ambulances are about to arrive, Handoff *calls the charge nurse* before the surge hits.

**1:00 – Live demo (45s)** → see `demo_script.md`

**1:45 – Impact & ask (15s)**
- Zero new hardware, zero app installs for EMS — it's a phone call.
- Built-in AB 40 compliance stats.
- "Handoff: get the ambulance back on the road."
